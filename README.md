# HYBRD — Train · Track · Fuel

Mobile-first, installable web app for hybrid athletes: calisthenics, strength, running, walking, outdoor cycling, indoor/stationary bike, cardio and HYROX.
Static site (Vite + React + TypeScript), works offline, accounts via Supabase (free). See **SETUP.md** to connect accounts and publish.

## Features
- **Hybrid Score** (0–100, last 7 days): Strength, Engine, Consistency and Fuel pillars, with a balance penalty; the formula is shown in-app. Streaks and PRs.
- **Train**: 59-exercise library with coaching cues; workout builder/logger (sets, reps, weight, time, distance, RPE, rest timer, templates); 10 programs including HYROX (8 × 1 km + 8 stations in the official order, division weights) and an Indoor / Stationary Bike program (recovery spin, endurance, 4 × 4 min intervals, HIIT sprints); history with split boards. **Levels**: a Beginner / Intermediate / Expert filter. The **Expert tier** has 5 programs (calisthenics levers/planche/weighted, Daniels-style running with E/M/T/I/R paces from a recent race, Coggan %FTP cycling indoor or outdoor, HYROX race sharpening at Open/Pro standards, and concurrent hybrid weeks), each with prerequisites, a readiness/safety note and cited sources. Interval sessions open a guided **Session** timer that shows each step's personal pace or watts; for-time sessions open a guided stopwatch where each Lap is a named piece. Tests: `node --experimental-strip-types --import ./tools/ts-resolve.mjs tools/test-expert.ts`. **Indoor Bike** and **Stationary Bike** start a time-based session with no GPS or map; at the end you can enter distance (mi/km), average speed, cadence (rpm), resistance and watts.
- **Record (GPS)**: run, walk and outdoor cycle with accuracy filtering, auto-pause, segments, splits, pace/speed, elevation gain, a Leaflet/OSM map (off by default to keep data use light), hold-to-finish, saved routes and a route history map. Indoor / stationary bike sessions on the same screen are timer-only (no map).
- **Local maps**: every map opens where the user is (a "you are here" dot with an accuracy circle, plus a Locate-me button). If location is off, maps fall back to the last known position (saved on the device only), then to a rough area from the device time zone, and show how to allow location. No city is hard-coded. Demo routes are drawn around the user's area.
- **Music (Spotify)**: public workout playlists for running, HIIT, lifting and walking, as Spotify embeds that need no account. **Connect Spotify** uses PKCE with no secret. It adds a mini player (track, art, play/pause, skip), a Spotify Connect device picker that plays on the phone's Spotify app, your playlists, and an optional in-browser player on desktop through the Web Playback SDK. Music controls are in Me › Music and on a **Music** button in Record, Timers and the workout logger. Voice cues lower the music while they speak. Setup is in SETUP.md §8. Playback control needs Spotify Premium, and in development mode the app is limited to 5 allow-listed users.
- **Devices & sync** (Me › Devices & sync):
  - **Bluetooth heart rate** uses Web Bluetooth's standard Heart Rate Service on Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet; iOS isn't supported. It shows live BPM and zones in Record, Timers and the logger, saves an HR chart with average and max, calculates HR-based calories (Keytel 2005) and reconnects automatically.
  - **Watch file import** reads .fit (with the MIT-licensed fit-file-parser), .tcx and .gpx files on the device. Several files can be added by drag-and-drop, and each gets a preview showing its route, HR, cadence, elevation, power and laps, plus duplicate detection. Indoor rides without GPS (and Strava VirtualRide / trainer rides) map to Indoor Bike. Sample files are in `public/samples/`.
  - **Strava sync** uses OAuth through a small token function you deploy yourself, so the secret is never on the site. It follows Strava's rules: data stays on the device, is shown only to the user and is kept for 7 days. Setup is in SETUP.md §9.
- **Timers**: stopwatch with laps, countdown, interval/HIIT, Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP and a HYROX sim timer. Based on timestamps so they survive a reload, with beeps, vibration (Android), optional voice and wake-lock.
- **Fuel**: daily calorie/macro log, an offline USDA SR Legacy list (7,283 foods), Open Food Facts search and barcode lookup, water tracker, 25 researched meals and drinks with USDA-computed macros, a nutrition guide, TDEE (Mifflin-St Jeor) MET-based burn estimates (indoor bike uses watts when entered, otherwise heart rate or Compendium stationary-cycling METs), and listed sources.
- **Burn (calories burned)** — `/burn`, in the desktop sidebar, from the **Calories burned** ring on Home, and linked from Fuel, Steps and Progress. Day / Week / Month views with an **Active | Total** toggle. **Active** = workouts (their saved estimate: device → power → Keytel HR → Compendium MET) + steps outside recorded workouts + calories added by hand. **Total** = resting + active, where resting is Mifflin-St Jeor BMR spread over the day (today: only the minutes so far), minus the minutes already inside workout and added-activity estimates, since those are gross. Steps use the ACSM walking equation, net of resting: 0.0005 × kg × step length (m) per step. Steps inside recorded walks/runs aren't counted again, and walks/runs with a distance but no step count are converted to steps so a phone's full-day total isn't double counted. Also: stacked daily charts, a per-activity breakdown, top sessions, a daily **active goal** (default 500 kcal, in the profile) with a Home progress ring and a goal streak, a **net balance** (Fuel eaten − total burned, only on days with food logged, labelled as an estimate), and manual entries (19 Compendium MET presets or your own MET, minutes and date, with the estimate shown and an optional override; edit or delete). Manual entries are a separate `burns` collection (Supabase table `burn_logs` with RLS), not workouts, so they don't change the Hybrid Score. Fuel's "kcal left" adds active burn only. The info sheet (ⓘ) lists the methods and sources. Tests: `node --experimental-strip-types --import ./tools/ts-resolve.mjs tools/test-burn.ts`. Screenshots: `node tools/shoot-burn.mjs` (with `node tools/serve.mjs` running).
- **Steps**: live step count and cadence on Walk/Run recordings from the phone's motion sensor (peak-detection pedometer), with a labelled GPS-distance estimate as fallback; daily Steps card (goal ring, add-to or set-day-total entry), 7/30-day chart, goal streak, best day, step calories (estimate, ACSM net per-step; see Burn) and a small Hybrid Score bonus. The web can't read Apple Health or Google Fit, so whole-day totals are copied in by hand.
- **Account**: sign up, log in, log out, change password, change email, forgot-password reset, sync. Clearly labeled Local mode until keys are added.
- **Units**: one-tap mi | km switch on Record (before or during a run; splits are rebuilt from the raw GPS points) and a Units card at the top of Me (mi/km plus a separate lb/kg). Every distance, pace, speed and elevation (ft/m) follows it instantly. Data is always stored in metric. Onboarding asks for units first, defaulting to miles for a US locale.
- Units mi/km and lb/kg, onboarding, JSON export/import, demo data, and an in-app help page (including the iOS limits).

Not medical advice.
