**HYBRD IS FOR GENERAL INFORMATION AND PERSONAL TRACKING ONLY. IT IS NOT MEDICAL, NUTRITIONAL, PSYCHOLOGICAL OR OTHER PROFESSIONAL ADVICE, AND IT DOES NOT DIAGNOSE, TREAT, CURE OR PREVENT ANY CONDITION.**

## Check with a professional first

Talk to a doctor before you start or change an exercise, nutrition, weight-loss or fasting program, especially if you:

- are pregnant or breastfeeding;
- are under 18;
- are older;
- have a heart, lung, metabolic, kidney or other medical condition;
- take medication;
- have diabetes;
- have, or have had, an eating disorder.

Never ignore medical advice or delay getting it because of something in HYBRD.

## Exercise

- Every workout, program, HYROX simulation, interval, ultra or backyard plan and coach suggestion is general. It may not suit you.
- Warm up, use good form and proper equipment, and progress gradually.
- **Stop at once** and get help if you feel chest pain, pressure, dizziness, fainting, unusual shortness of breath, an irregular heartbeat or severe pain.
- Outdoors:
  - follow traffic laws and stay aware of cars, cyclists, terrain, weather and heat;
  - be visible at night;
  - carry ID and water;
  - tell someone your route;
  - never use the app in a way that distracts you while moving near traffic.

## Nutrition, calories and fasting

- Calorie, macro and water targets are estimates from published equations. Your real needs can be very different.
- Food data comes from the USDA FoodData Central database (a public-domain US government source), Open Food Facts (an open community database) and what you enter. It can contain errors.
- Always read labels for allergens.
- Fasting and fast weight loss are not safe for everyone. HYBRD's safety screens can turn features off, but they do not replace a doctor.
- Very low calorie intake, dehydration, and drinking too much water during long events (hyponatremia) can be dangerous.

## Numbers can be wrong

GPS distance, pace, route and elevation, heart rate, calories burned, steps, scores, sleep estimates and predictions all depend on sensors and formulas. They can be inaccurate. Do not rely on them for medical or safety decisions.

## Mind & Recovery and Pulse

- Check-ins, mood scores and questionnaires are self-reflection tools, not clinical assessments. A result is not a diagnosis.
- Breathing, cold, heat and recovery sessions have their own risks; read their safety notes.
- If you are struggling, talk to a licensed professional.
- **If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 in the US (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 or your local emergency number.** Outside the US, see findahelpline.com.
