## Filing a notice

Your notice must include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the copyrighted work you say is infringed (or a list, if there are several);
3. the material you say infringes it, with enough detail for us to find it (for example the link or handle, and a description of the post, story, comment or message);
4. your name, address, phone number and email;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, **under penalty of perjury**, that you are the owner or are authorised to act for the owner.

Knowingly false claims can make you liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f). Consider whether the use might be fair use before you send a notice.

## What we do

When we receive a valid notice, we remove or disable access to the material promptly and tell the person who posted it.

## Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake or a misidentification, send our agent a counter-notice with:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the material that was removed and where it appeared;
3. a statement **under penalty of perjury** that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and phone number;
5. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the US, any judicial district where we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the notice.

We will forward your counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. We will restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless they tell us they have filed a court action to stop the infringement.

## Repeat infringers

We will terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright. Normally that means accounts with three valid notices that were not successfully countered.
