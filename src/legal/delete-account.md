# Account & Data Deletion

**App:** HYBRD · **Developer:** José Manuel Cruz Ortiz, an individual doing business as HYBRD · **Effective date:** November 26, 2026 · **Last updated:** November 26, 2026

This page explains how to delete your HYBRD account and data, what is deleted, and what is kept.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open HYBRD and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings › Account › Delete account** (or open [/safety/delete](/safety/delete)).
3. Optionally download your data first.
4. Enter your password and confirm.

## No longer have the app, or can't sign in?

Email **hybrdbeast@gmail.com** from the email address on your account. Use the subject **"Delete my HYBRD account"**.

We will confirm the request and delete the account as described below. We may ask you to confirm from that address so we know the request is yours.

## What happens and when

- **Right away:** your account is deactivated. Your profile, posts, stories, comments and shared activities are hidden, and people cannot message or call you.
- **For 30 days:** you can sign in and tap **Reopen account** to restore everything.
- **After 30 days:** we permanently delete:
  - your account and login;
  - your profile;
  - your training, nutrition, body, sleep and mind logs;
  - your GPS routes;
  - your posts, stories, comments, reactions and shared activities;
  - the messages you sent;
  - your photos, videos and voice notes in our storage.

## What we keep

- **Reports and moderation records.** These are kept only as long as needed for safety, appeals and legal duties, and are not linked to your account after deletion.
- **Information we must keep by law,** or to protect someone's safety.
- **Encrypted backups,** for up to 30 days until they are overwritten.

## Deleting some data without deleting your account

- Delete any log, post, story, comment or message from its menu.
- Unsend a message for everyone within 24 hours.
- Use **Me › Your data › Delete everything** to erase all logs and photos at once.
- In Settings, disconnect Strava or Spotify. This removes their tokens and cached data from your device.

## Local mode (no account)

Your data is only on your device. Delete it with **Me › Your data › Delete everything**, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
