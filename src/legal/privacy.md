# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026 · **Last updated:** November 26, 2026 · **Version:** 2026-11-26

This policy explains what José Manuel Cruz Ortiz, an individual doing business as HYBRD ("HYBRD", "we") collects through the HYBRD app and website at https://cryp2x1.github.io/hybrd/, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Health-related data has its own page, the [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/health-data).

## The short version

- **No ads, no selling, no sharing for ads.** We do not sell your personal information. We do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not show ads. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
- **Local mode keeps everything on your device.** You can use most of HYBRD without an account. Your logs then stay in your browser's storage and we never receive them.
- **With an account,** your data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase), protected by row-level security, so only you can read your private logs.
- **Location and health data need your OK.** We ask before using precise location and before collecting mood and mental-wellbeing data. You can withdraw that consent in **Settings › Privacy**.
- **You control deletion.** Delete individual items at any time. **Delete account** deactivates your account at once and permanently erases it after **30 days** (you can reopen it before then).

## 1. Information we collect

**You give us:**

- **Account:** your email address and password. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth, and we never see them. We also keep the version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and when.
- **Profile and settings:** your first name, sex (for calorie formulas), age or date of birth, height, weight, activity level, goals, training disciplines, units and preferences. If you use Friends & Stories, also your handle, display name, public bio, profile photo and privacy choices. Your date of birth is private and is used only for age checks.
- **Training:** workouts, sets, timers, programs, coach plans, HYROX and ultra plans, steps, burn logs, gear, and imported activity files (GPX/FIT).
- **Location:** GPS routes recorded while you use Record (only while recording, with your permission), your privacy zones and route-hiding choices, and a last-known position kept on your device to centre maps.
- **Health and body:**
  - weight and body measurements;
  - heart rate (from a Bluetooth chest strap or imported files);
  - nutrition, calorie, water, fasting and sleep logs;
  - weight-loss plans;
  - Mind & Recovery check-ins, Pulse mood scores, optional questionnaires (such as PHQ- or GAD-style questions), journal entries, and safety-screen answers.

  See the [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/health-data).
- **Social content:** posts, stories, comments, reactions, saved and hidden posts, friend requests, blocks, restrictions, and shared activities (with the audience you choose). Photos are re-encoded on your device before upload, which removes embedded location (EXIF) data.
- **Messages and calls:**
  - **Messages:** the text, photos, videos and voice notes you send, plus reactions, read receipts and typing indicators.
  - **Calls:** call records (who called whom, when, and how long the call lasted).
  - **We do not record calls.** Voice and video travel directly between devices (peer-to-peer WebRTC), encrypted in transit. When a direct connection is not possible, they may be relayed in encrypted form through a TURN relay server, which cannot see the content.
  - **Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.** They are encrypted in transit and stored on our hosting provider's servers.
- **Safety:** reports you make (including copies of the reported content or messages), blocks and restrictions, appeals, time-off and deletion requests, and moderation decisions about you.
- **Support:** what you send us when you contact us.

**Collected automatically:**

- **Technical data needed to run the Service:**
  - your IP address and browser or device information, which our hosting providers process in their server logs;
  - timestamps of syncs and sign-ins;
  - error responses.

  We do not run analytics software, and we do not build advertising profiles.
- **On your device (local storage, not cookies):** see Section 9.

**From third parties, only if you connect them:**

- **Strava:** activities you choose to import. They stay on your device and are not uploaded to your HYBRD account.
- **Spotify:** your playlists and playback controls. Tokens stay on your device.
- **Food databases:** nutrition facts for foods you look up.

## 2. How we use information

We use your information to:

- provide the features you ask for (logging, maps, plans, targets, sync, social, messages, calls);
- keep accounts secure;
- prevent spam, abuse and fraud;
- enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and review reports and appeals;

We do **not** use your health, mood or location data for advertising, and we do not use it to infer things about you beyond the features you use. Calorie targets, scores and plans are calculated mostly on your device.

## 3. How we share information

We share personal information only:

- **With other users, as you choose.** This includes your public or friends-only profile, posts, stories, comments, shared activities (with start and end hidden if you choose), and messages and calls with the people you contact. People you share with can copy or screenshot what they see.
- **With service providers (processors)** who handle data for us under contracts that restrict their use of it:
  - **Supabase:** database, authentication, file storage, realtime and server functions;
  - **GitHub Pages:** hosting of the web app;
  - **your chosen TURN relay provider** for calls that cannot connect directly;
  - **email delivery** for account emails.
- **When you use third-party services directly.** These see your IP address and request because your device contacts them:
  - map tiles from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap;
  - food lookups from Open Food Facts;
  - public STUN servers (for example Google STUN) to set up calls;
  - Strava and Spotify if you connect them;
  - embedded Spotify players.

  Their own privacy policies apply.
- **For legal and safety reasons:**
  - to comply with law or valid legal process;
  - to protect someone's life or safety;
  - to report child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as required by law;
  - to enforce our Terms;
  - to protect our rights.
- **In a business transfer,** such as a merger or sale, subject to this policy.
- **With your consent.**

**We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising,** and have not done so in the last 12 months.

## 4. Sensitive information and your consent

Precise geolocation, health data, mental-health information, account login details and the content of your messages are "sensitive" under several state laws.

- We use sensitive information **only to provide the features you request** and for the security, safety and legal purposes allowed by law.
- **Precise location:** we ask for your consent in the app (and your device asks for permission) before recording GPS. You can say no and still use indoor logging. Shared routes can hide your start and end, and your privacy zones.
- **Mood and mental-wellbeing data:** collected only after you opt in on the Mind & Recovery screen.
- **Consumer health data** is covered by the [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/health-data).
- **Withdrawing consent:** you can withdraw either consent in **Settings › Privacy › Data consents**. This stops new collection. To erase what was already collected, delete the items or your account.

## 5. Children and teens

HYBRD is for people **18 and older**. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

- The app checks age with a date of birth for Friends & Stories, and blocks under-13 sign-ups.
- If we learn that we collected information from a child under 13, or from anyone under our minimum age, we will delete it.
- Parents can contact hybrdbeast@gmail.com.

If a user under 18 reaches the Service despite our minimum age, extra protections apply:

- private-by-default profiles;
- stricter messaging and call defaults;
- longer route trimming.

These protections do not mean we accept users under our minimum age.

## 6. How long we keep information

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Your logs, profile and content | Until you delete them or your account |
| Stories | Shown for 24 hours; the server deletes them about a day after they expire |
| Messages you "unsend for everyone" (possible within 24 hours) | Removed for everyone; "delete for me" hides them only for you |
| Deleted account | Deactivated at once; after **30 days**, your profile, logs, posts, stories, comments, shared activities, the messages you sent and your stored files are permanently deleted |
| Reports and moderation records | Kept as long as needed for safety, appeals and legal duties; after your account is deleted they are no longer linked to your account |
| Backups | Your hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for up to 30 days; deleted data ages out of them |
| Local-mode and on-device data | Stays in your browser until you delete it (Me › Your data) or clear site data |

Copies of messages you sent to other people are deleted from their chats when your account is purged. Screenshots or copies they made themselves are outside our control.

## 7. Security and breach notice

We use:

- encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS);
- encryption at rest by our hosting provider;
- row-level security, so other users cannot read your private rows;
- short-lived access tokens;
- server-side rate limits;
- private storage buckets.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law. This includes the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state breach laws.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

HYBRD does **not** set advertising or analytics cookies. To work, the app stores data in your browser:

- **localStorage, sessionStorage and IndexedDB** hold:
  - your sign-in session (Supabase Auth);
  - local-mode data and offline copies of your logs;
  - settings such as theme;
  - drafts and outgoing message queues;
  - demo data;
  - connection tokens for Strava and Spotify, if you connect them;
  - your last map position.
- A **service worker** caches the app so it works offline.

These are strictly necessary for features you use. You can clear them in your browser settings, which signs you out and deletes local-mode data.

Embedded third-party content, such as Spotify players and map tiles, may set its own cookies under that provider's policy.

**Do Not Track:** we do not track you across third-party websites, and we treat all users the same whether or not a browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. If we ever sell data or do targeted advertising, we will honour Global Privacy Control as an opt-out.
