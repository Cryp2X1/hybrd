# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026 · **Last updated:** November 26, 2026 · **Version:** 2026-11-26

This policy covers "consumer health data" collected by José Manuel Cruz Ortiz, an individual doing business as HYBRD through HYBRD, as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act.

## 1. Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

| Category | How it is used |
|---|---|
| Body measurements: weight, height, age, sex, body logs | Calorie and macro targets, weight trends, heart-rate zones and calorie-burn estimates |
| Fitness activity: workouts, GPS-based pace and effort, steps, active calories, training load | Your training log, scores, plans and coach adjustments |
| Heart rate (live from a Bluetooth strap, or from imported files) | Showing heart rate, zones and effort during and after workouts |
| Nutrition: foods, calories, macros, water, fasting windows, weight-loss plans | Fuel logging, targets, fasting timers and plans |
| Sleep logs and sleep-plan answers | Sleep tracking and evening routines |
| Mental wellbeing: Pulse mood scores, check-ins, optional questionnaires (such as PHQ- or GAD-style questions), journal entries, safety-screen answers | Showing your trends, suggesting recovery or breathing sessions, and showing crisis resources when answers suggest risk |
| Health-related safety answers in Fasting, Sleep, Ultra and Mind setup (for example pregnancy, diabetes or eating-disorder history) | Turning off or adjusting features that could be unsafe for you |
| Precise location tied to workouts | Recording routes, distance and pace |

We use this data only to provide the features you request, to keep the Service secure, and to comply with the law.

**Consent:** we collect mental-wellbeing data only after you opt in on the Mind & Recovery screen, and precise location only after you opt in on Record. Other health data is collected only when you enter or connect it, because it is necessary to provide the feature you asked for.

## 2. Sources

- **You,** when you type or select it.
- **Your devices,** such as a GPS phone or a Bluetooth heart-rate strap.
- **Files you import** (GPX/FIT).
- **Services you connect:** Strava activities stay on your device only.

## 3. Categories of consumer health data we share

We do **not** sell consumer health data.

**Shared only to provide the Service:**

- the health data you store in your account, stored by our processor;
- the workouts, routes and stats you choose to post or share with other users, with the audience you pick.

**Never shared:** mood, check-in, questionnaire and journal data. You can keep these on your device only.

## 4. Third parties and affiliates we share with

- **Processor:** Supabase, Inc. (database, authentication, storage and server functions), acting on our instructions.
- **Other HYBRD users,** only for content you choose to share.
- **Authorities, where required by law** or to protect someone's life or safety.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

## 5. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

- confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of the third parties and affiliates it was shared with (use **Me › Your data › Export**, or email us);
- withdraw consent (**Settings › Privacy › Data consents**);
- delete it: delete items, use **Me › Your data › Delete everything**, or delete your account. Deletion also reaches our processor and backups as they cycle.

**To make a request,** email hybrdbeast@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "Health data request".

- We respond within 45 days. We may extend this once by 45 days, and we will tell you if we do.
- To appeal a decision, reply with "Appeal" in the subject. We will answer within 45 days. If we deny your appeal, you may contact the Washington, Nevada or Connecticut Attorney General.
